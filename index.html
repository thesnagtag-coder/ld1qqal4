LIMITS
  • What a seller must disclose is set by local law and differs between countries and states. Ask your agent or conveyancer what applies to you. (Professional practice)
  • Record known issues honestly, even after a repair. Hiding a defect can create problems later; a documented repair with an invoice builds trust. (Professional practice)
  • Safety items (gas, electrical, structural) are for licensed or registered professionals. This pack only records what was seen and done. (Professional practice)
